# The story

The first hour of BocciaBound, and the frame the rest hangs on.

Told through play, short dialogue, journal pages and the world itself. **Never
through walls of text.** Three lines is the most anyone says in one go. All of
it goes through a strings table so it can ship in Portuguese and English.

## The frame

Aerenfall is split by **the Abyss**, a chasm so deep nobody has seen the
bottom. On its rim sits **Beiral**: a small village of delvers, traders and
farmers who live off what the chasm gives up.

Once a generation the Abyss exhales. People call it **the Rise**. For one night
creatures from the deep climb out, confused and hungry, and the village holds or
it does not. Lately the Rise comes more often, and each one is stronger than the
last.

**You are the child of Maren**, the best delver Beiral ever had. Three years ago
she went down to find out why the Rise was getting worse. She never came back. A
week later her lantern came up on her rope, still lit. It is by your bed.

You pick your own name.

## The tone

Quiet, warm, a little sad on the surface. Beautiful and wrong below.

*Quanto mais fundo, mais estranho.* The deeper, the stranger.

**You are not the chosen one.** Nobody says you are the only hope. The village
has its own problems and its own opinions about Maren's child going down the
same hole. The world does not revolve around you; this is a personal story
happening inside an indifferent one.

## The first hour, beat by beat

Each beat names what the player should **want**, what should **stop** them, and
what they should **feel**. A beat without all three is not designed yet.

### 1. Morning in Beiral

Playable from the first second. No cutscene.

You wake. The lantern is on the table, lit. You walk out into golden light.
**Bento**, the old outfitter who was Maren's partner, has a job: bring back some
wood, and he will show you how to make a pickaxe.

- **Want:** to do the favour, because he asked and he knew your mother
- **Stops them:** nothing. This is the tutorial wearing a coat
- **Feel:** warmth, and a small ache about the lantern

### 2. The pup

Last night's tremor opened a cave in the forest. Inside, a wolf pup is trapped
under fallen rock beside its mother, who died fighting something that should not
have been up here: an **Abyssling**.

You dig the pup out. It is frightened and it snaps at you. It will not simply
follow you: you have to earn it. Cook meat. Put it down. Back away. Wait. Come
closer than last time. The trust bar fills.

When it trusts you it follows you home and sleeps by your fire.

- **Want:** the pup to stop being afraid of you
- **Stops them:** it does not want you near, and rushing sets it back
- **Feel:** patience rewarded, and the first thing in the game that is *yours*

**This is the emotional core of the slice. It gets the most polish of anything.**

### 3. The first page

Near the rim, a page of Maren's journal is pinned under a stone.

> *If you're reading this, don't follow me. If you do anyway, bring something
> that can see in the dark.*

- **Want:** to know what happened to her
- **Stops them:** you have no light worth the name
- **Feel:** told not to, and going anyway

### 4. The caverns

Torches burn out. In the wet caverns lives the **Sapo Luminoso**, a glowing
frog. It likes glowbugs. Bond with it and it rides your shoulder and lights
your way.

Now you have two companions and can only bring one. That is the first real
choice the game asks: the wolf that fights, or the frog that sees.

- **Want:** to go deeper than a torch allows
- **Stops them:** the dark, and having to leave one friend at home
- **Feel:** properly equipped for the first time

### 5. The first descent

Abyss layer 1. The music changes. Purple light, mushrooms taller than you,
movement at the edge of the lamplight. It is beautiful and it wants to kill you.

You find the second journal page, and Maren's old camp, where you build your
first **beacon** so you do not have to walk the whole way again.

- **Want:** to follow her trail
- **Stops them:** everything down here
- **Feel:** awe first, fear second, in that order

### 6. The guardian

At the bottom of layer 2, a large Abyssling has made a nest of crystal. Three
phases, readable wind-ups, about three minutes if you came prepared. It hides in
the dark, so the frog's light matters; it closes hard, so the wolf's bite
matters; it telegraphs, so your feet matter.

Winning gives the material for your first real armour.

- **Want:** to get past it
- **Stops them:** it is genuinely harder than anything so far
- **Feel:** that the last hour of preparation was the fight

### 7. The Rise

You climb out into a red sky. The Rise has come early. Creatures pour over the
rim and into Beiral, and you hold the village with Bento and whoever else can
still stand. Your wolf, grown now, is strong enough to ride for the first time.

- **Want:** the village to still be there in the morning
- **Stops them:** waves, in the dark, on ground you have to defend
- **Feel:** that this is what everything was for

### 8. The hook

At dawn the village stands. You go home. Maren's lantern, by your bed,
flickers, and the flame leans over.

Toward the Abyss.

- **Feel:** she is down there, and now you know you are going

## Cast

| Who | What they are | How they talk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Maren** | Your mother. Absent, present in everything | Only in journal pages, in her own hand |
| **Bento** | Outfitter, Maren's old partner | Gruff, kind, short sentences, never sentimental out loud |
| **Lia** | Cook and trader | Warm, practical, asks after you |
| **Tomas** | Lookout on the rim | Nervous, full of rumours, usually half right |

## Rules for writing it

- **Three lines maximum** in one exchange. If it needs more, it is a journal
  page or it happens in play.
- **Nobody explains the plot.** Bento does not tell you the Rise is getting
  worse; Tomas says he counted fewer days than last time and lets you do the
  arithmetic.
- **Maren is never shown.** Only her handwriting, her lantern, her rope, her
  camp, and what people say when they think you cannot hear.
- **The village has opinions about you going down.** Not all of them kind.
- **Every line in the strings table**, keyed, so Portuguese and English ship
  together and the system language picks.
